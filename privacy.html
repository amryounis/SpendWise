SpendWise Privacy Policy

Simple, honest, and on your device.

🔒 No account required  ·  No data leaves your phone

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately upon installation

SpendWise is designed from the ground up to keep your financial data private. Everything stays on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

📋 Overview

SpendWise is a personal expense tracking app for Android. It allows you to capture and organise your spending using receipt photos, voice notes, free text, or manual entry.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app uses, how it is stored, and your rights as a user. By using SpendWise, you agree to the practices described here.

📵 Data We Do Not Collect

SpendWise does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Behavioural analytics or usage statistics
  • Financial account numbers or bank details
  • SMS messages or contacts
  • Any data from other apps on your device

We have no backend server that receives or stores your expense data.

📱 Data Stored On Your Device

All expense data you enter — including merchants, amounts, dates, receipt photos, voice transcripts, and categories — is stored locally in a database on your device (SQLite). This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly choose to export it.

You can delete all stored data at any time via Settings → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data.

🎙️ Voice and Camera Permissions

Microphone: Used only when you actively record a voice note. Audio is processed on-device using Android's built-in speech recognition. No audio is recorded passively or transmitted to any server.

Camera and Photo Library: Used only when you choose to scan a receipt. Image processing (OCR) happens entirely on-device. Photos are not stored by the app after processing is complete unless you save the resulting expense.

📤 Data Export

SpendWise offers a voluntary CSV and Excel export feature. When you use this feature, a file is generated on your device and shared via Android's standard share sheet. You choose where to send it. We have no involvement in or visibility into that transfer.

🔥 Firebase

SpendWise includes Firebase libraries as a technical dependency for future Pro tier features. In the current Mobile version, Firebase does not collect or transmit your expense data. A basic network initialisation request may be made on app launch to confirm connectivity — this contains no personal or financial information.

🛒 In-App Purchase

SpendWise Advanced is available as a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Payment information is handled entirely by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. SpendWise does not receive or store your payment details.

👶 Children's Privacy

SpendWise is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will assist with data removal.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
